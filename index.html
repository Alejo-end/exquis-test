Exquis key test

Press every pad and button, and turn every encoder both ways and push it. Each control turns from red to green, here and on the Exquis, when it sends. Then press each pad hard: a yellow ring marks a pad that reached full pressure.

Needs Chrome or Edge, and the Exquis on USB. Nothing leaves your computer. Close the Exquis app and any music software first, because only one program can hold the Exquis.

Not connected.

Here the Exquis plays as itself. Put it in MPE mode first: hold the encoder to change the mode, and the LED shows blue in MPE. Hit notes soft and hard for velocity. Then rock each finger without lifting it: tilt it left and right for X (pitch bend), tilt it toward you and away for Y (CC 74), and press down for Z (pressure). Each check turns green when the Exquis has sent its full range.

If X stays at 0, the per-note pitch bend range in the Exquis's settings may be 0. If every velocity is the same, its velocity curve may be fixed.
